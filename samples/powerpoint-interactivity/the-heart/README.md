# Interactive Human Heart Reveal
Create an interactive, full-bleed slide featuring a **draggable external/internal anatomical reveal of a human heart**.

## Core Visual
Use **AI-generated medical visualization**, not vector art.

Create a highly realistic 3D human heart:

- Isolated with transparency
- Viewed slightly from the front
- Major vessels and overall shape clearly visible
- Photorealistic anatomical detail
- Natural anatomical coloring
- Soft studio-style lighting that clearly reveals three-dimensional form
Place the heart prominently in the center of the slide.

Use a subtle, dark clinical background with soft depth and no competing subjects.

The composition should be **edge-to-edge with no margins**.

---

## Interactive Reveal
Add a single vertical divider with a small circular drag handle.

The user must be able to drag the divider freely left and right to reveal two perfectly synchronized views of the **exact same heart**.

### Left Side — External Anatomy
Show the complete photorealistic exterior of the heart, including:

- Major vessels
- Surface anatomy and texture
- Coronary vessels
- Natural anatomical coloring

### Right Side — Internal Anatomy
Show the same heart with:

- Identical size
- Identical position
- Identical rotation
- Identical lighting
- Identical animation timing
The only difference should be that the outer wall has been cleanly removed to reveal the internal anatomy.

Clearly depict:

- Left atrium
- Right atrium
- Left ventricle
- Right ventricle
- Interventricular septum
- Mitral valve
- Tricuspid valve
- Aortic valve
- Pulmonary valve
- Chordae tendineae
- Major vessel openings
The cutaway should feel like a true anatomical section of the same heart, **not a separate illustration**.

---

## Divider Behavior
The draggable divider should function like an **anatomical X-ray reveal**.

As the divider moves across the heart:

- Transition seamlessly between intact exterior and internal cutaway
- Keep all anatomical structures perfectly aligned across the divider
- Keep both views synchronized at all times
- Allow inspection of any portion of the outside versus inside anatomy
- Continue the heartbeat animation while the divider is being dragged
Interaction should feel smooth, immediate, and responsive.

---

## Heartbeat Animation
Animate the heart continuously at approximately **65–75 beats per minute**.

Use a realistic contraction-and-relaxation cycle rather than simple exaggerated scaling.

The animation should include:

- Visible ventricular contraction during systole
- Ventricular relaxation during diastole
- Subtle natural motion of the atria
- Slight movement of the major vessels
The external and internal versions must use **exactly the same animation values, timing, and phase** so they remain perfectly synchronized across the divider.

### Internal Valve Animation
On the internal view, subtly animate the chamber walls and valves.

During ventricular contraction:

- Mitral valve closes
- Tricuspid valve closes
- Aortic valve opens
- Pulmonary valve opens
During ventricular relaxation:

- Reverse these motions appropriately
Keep the movement medically plausible while still being visually understandable in a presentation.

---

## Optional Blood-Flow Visualization
Optionally add a subtle blood-flow visualization to the internal view using:

- Small semi-transparent moving particles, or
- Soft flowing highlights

### Deoxygenated Blood
Use **blue** to show blood:

1. Entering the right atrium
2. Moving into the right ventricle
3. Exiting through the pulmonary artery

### Oxygenated Blood
Use **red** to show blood:

1. Entering through the pulmonary veins
2. Moving into the left atrium
3. Moving into the left ventricle
4. Exiting through the aorta
Keep blood-flow effects secondary to the anatomical visualization.

---

## Text

### Heading
**Inside the Human Heart**

Visually emphasize the words **Human Heart**.

### View Labels
Add small labels:

- **External Anatomy** on the left
- **Internal Anatomy** on the right

### Bottom Caption

> ◀ Drag to explore inside the heart ▶
Do not add additional explanatory paragraphs or visual clutter.

## Visual Style
The overall design should resemble a **premium interactive anatomy application** suitable for:

- High-school education
- University instruction
- Professional presentations
Style requirements:

- Sophisticated
- Photorealistic
- Medically credible
- Clean and modern
- Visually impressive without being sensational
Avoid:

- Cartoon-like anatomy
- Excessive gore
- Surgical imagery
- Graphic tissue
- Excessive blood
- Decorative effects that distract from the anatomy

---

## Lighting and Alignment
Use soft studio-style directional lighting.

The external and internal heart renderings must have:

- Identical lighting direction
- Identical shadows
- Identical perspective
- Identical camera position
- Identical scale
- Identical rotation
Alignment across the draggable divider must remain precise.

---

## Responsiveness
The experience must scale cleanly to different slide sizes.

Requirements:

- No clipping
- No scrollbars
- Heart remains centered
- Full heart remains visible
- Major vessels remain visible
- Works particularly well at standard **16:9 presentation dimensions**

---

## Performance
Optimize the interactive asset for smooth animation and dragging.

Where appropriate:

- Use compressed WebP or similarly efficient image assets
- Minimize asset size
- Keep the total payload as small as practical
- Preserve enough anatomical detail for large-screen presentation
- Prioritize responsive dragging and smooth heartbeat animation
